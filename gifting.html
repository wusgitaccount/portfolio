welcome! This page is an archive of my collections of wrapping paper & gifting. "The Japanese equate wrapping a gift with “wrapping the heart,” so every gift is marked by thoughtfulness and consideration, both for the object housed within the container or wrapping, and for the recipient of the gift." Wrapping has been a long time passion of mine, an important ritual act that mirrors my heart.

On the 3rd of February 2024, I found a $1 reserve Trademe listing for a container full of wrapping paper.

Following a successful day at Goat Island on the 6th of February, my friends helped me pick up the bid. The entire boot was full of wrapping paper, and we had to carry it on the backseat as well.
